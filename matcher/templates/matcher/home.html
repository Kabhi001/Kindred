how edmonds' blossom algorithm guarantees cohort harmony.

Most systems match greedily: student A takes the highest match student B, leaving student C with terrible compatibility student D. Kindred uses Jack Edmonds' Blossom Maximum-Weight Matching to find the global optimum pairing that maximizes overall satisfaction across every student simultaneously.

Blossom Contraction

Shrinks odd-length alternating cycles into pseudonodes, preventing combinatorial explosion.

Deal-Breaker Pruning

FREQUENTLY ASKED

questions & legal governance.

Is my lifestyle quiz data shared with everyone?

No. In strict compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, raw response numbers are never leaked. Peers only see mutual affinity bands and normalized radar petals.

What happens if my requested roommate declines?

Pairing requests expire after 48 hours or upon decline. You can immediately send a new request to another peer, or participate in the automated Blossom matching round.

Are smokers allowed in university residences?

All university residential hostels are strictly 100% smoke-free and tobacco-free. Smoking or vaping in rooms carries immediate eviction and allotment cancellation.

Can freshers be allotted with seniors?

Under statutory university anti-ragging directives, freshman students are segregated into dedicated blocks and are mathematically blocked from senior pairings.
