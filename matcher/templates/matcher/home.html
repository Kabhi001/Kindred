common questions

how it all works.

How does the student OTP sign-in work? +
Students sign in using their registered roll number or college email. A 6-digit cryptographic OTP is generated, hashed with PBKDF2 in the database, and valid for 5 minutes (max 3 entry attempts). In demo mode, the code is shown instantly in the toast for seamless testing.
What is a deal-breaker? +
When taking the lifestyle quiz, you can mark questions (like smoking or sleep hours) as a deal-breaker. If a classmate has an incompatible habit, Kindred flags the clash and caps your mutual compatibility score at 59%, protecting you from incompatible rooms.
How does physical check-in work? +
When rooms are officially published, visit your hostel caretaker desk. The caretaker logs into the Caretaker Facility Desk, verifies your roll number, marks your physical bed arrival, and documents your room key handover.
